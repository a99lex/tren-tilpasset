Training with jumper’s knee

How to adapt workouts around jumper’s knee, also called patellar tendinopathy.

Jumper’s knee often causes pain around the tendon below the kneecap. Jumping and other movements that load the tendon heavily can aggravate symptoms.

Gradual strengthening can be part of rehabilitation. Follow the loading plan you agreed with your physiotherapist. Choose Knee in the planner to find general activity ideas that avoid loading the knee.
