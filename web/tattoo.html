Working out with a new tattoo

How to adjust exercise while a new tattoo heals.

A fresh forearm tattoo can rub against clothing, a barbell or a bench. Follow your tattoo artist’s aftercare instructions and wait on exercises that put pressure on the area or rub against it.

If your aftercare instructions allow it, a short, easy walk is an option. Seek medical advice if redness or warmth increases, or if you develop pus or fever.
